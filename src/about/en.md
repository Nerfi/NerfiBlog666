## An unconventional blog, by an unconventional person.

### Why does TRIP exist?

**This blog comes from an idea I have had for a long time, something I always wanted to have but always found an excuse not to build. Now, with AI (cursed by many, blessed by others), I have no excuse left: I can hand the building of the blog to an agent and focus only on writing down my thoughts, or whatever comes to mind. Maybe nobody will read this, which is the likely outcome, but hey! I finally get rid of the thorn that has been stuck in my side about this blog.**

*In case you are reading this and wondering who is behind TRIP: just a regular guy from a regular neighbourhood who once dreamed of travelling and, at 18, grabbed a suitcase and moved abroad. He could not speak English and had only the money from selling his moped. Since then he has tried never to stop travelling, meeting people and having adventures. You know how it goes: you only get one life, and it is better to die with memories than with regrets. A kiss, TRIP.*
